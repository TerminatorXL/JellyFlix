**1.0.0.0** — first release.

- Films and series: Polish title, original title, synopsis, genres, production countries, premiere
  date, the users' rating as the community rating and the critics' as the critic rating.
- Directors and cast with photos. Filmweb does not publish character names, so actors come without
  their roles.
- The Polish poster, and stills offered as backdrops.
- A **Filmweb** field in the Identify dialog that takes an id or a pasted page address, and a link
  to the title's page among the item's external links.
